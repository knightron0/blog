I want to live a life full of creation.

There are so many things I'd like to create. I want to make videos [like this](https://youtu.be/1igD-72dLH4?si=PnyFTB4Besx1pDfU). I want to write a fiction book someday, though I have no idea what about yet. I want to put together a photography book, filled with moments that bring back memories for the people close to me. I want to design and build a house. I want to paint something that hangs on the walls of my own home someday. I want to build tools that [expand what people are capable](/blog/problems-im-thinking-about#abstractions-and-interfaces) of creating. I want to contribute [something new](/blog/problems-im-thinking-about#future) to our understanding of the world.

I'm excited by the possibility of a passing interest or hobby eventually becoming something I can hold, watch, read, or send to a friend. I might discover that I enjoy making videos [much more](https://x.com/msarthak29/status/2097090501694619777?a=3oIg1zIaJD_WJIurdt8&s=20) than I expected, or stumble upon an entirely new form of creation. I'm sure there are things I'll want to make that I can't even think of today. I like not knowing what those might be.

When I imagine looking back on my life, I like the thought of having traces of who I was scattered across it. Things that remind me of the people I knew, the questions I was asking, and what I found fascinating at the time. In a way, they'd be little relics that help me stay in touch with that version of myself. Some I’d still be proud of; most I’d probably find embarrassing.

I think I’d be glad I made them.
